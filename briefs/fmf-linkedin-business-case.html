Questions, answered

The things every CMO asks before deciding.

If your question is not here, ask Tom on the call. We try not to bury anything.

Why are you approaching us if we are not even running ads?
Because the absence is the signal. We run the LinkedIn Ad Library against B2B categories where the channel pays back and your category is moving. Your competitors are showing up to your buyers right now and you are not — that is the gap we want to size with you. We make no claim that LinkedIn is the right channel for every B2B business. The business case is how we both find out before any commitment.
What is the "business case" exactly?
A one-page PDF showing your specific projection: monthly spend, projected LinkedIn-influenced opportunities over 6 months, year-1 revenue, customer-lifetime revenue, payback period, and the source benchmarks for every number. Formatted to walk straight into a CFO meeting. We send it within 24 hours of you submitting the form, personalised to your inputs. It is yours to use even if you decide we are not the right partner.
How much spend do I need to commit to start?
£5k/month is the floor for the targeting maths to work — below that, LinkedIn's audience size constraints make precision targeting unreliable. Most of our work starts at £10k/month, where unit economics materially improve. The business case shows you exactly what each spend level delivers so you can pick the level that fits your finance reality.
Do you require a long contract?
No. If we do move forward, our standard 6-month engagement includes a 30-day exit clause from month 3. Either side can end the engagement with no further fees due. Ad spend already committed to LinkedIn cannot be refunded by us, but you keep all audiences, creative, attribution dashboards and learnings produced to that point.
How is your approach different from a typical LinkedIn agency?
Three differences. (1) Official LinkedIn Marketing Partner — the only one in EMEA, vetted by LinkedIn against expertise, delivery quality and outcomes at scale. (2) We measure on LinkedIn-influenced opportunities tied to your CRM and attribution platform, not on engagement vanity metrics. (3) Paid activation runs alongside a LinkedIn outreach layer and conversion-optimised landing pages — one connected system, not a media-buying silo. Tom can walk you through how that works on the call.
What happens after I submit my business case?
Tom personally reads every submission. Within 24 hours you receive the formatted PDF in your inbox along with a short, human note. If the numbers look right and you want to talk, you book a 15-min call directly from the email. If the numbers do not look right, we will tell you that too — and suggest what to do next. No drip campaigns, no SDR follow-up sequences.
Can we start with a smaller test budget?
Below £5k/month the targeting precision LinkedIn requires breaks down — you cannot reliably reach a defined buying committee at scale. We would rather tell you that than take your money and underdeliver. If the £5k floor is not realistic for H2 2026, the most useful version of this conversation is timing — not commitment.
Why six months — can we run shorter?
For a cold start on LinkedIn, six months is the minimum window for paid activation to compound: month 1 builds the foundation (audiences, creative, baseline), months 2 to 4 test multivariant approaches, months 5 to 6 scale what works. Anything shorter and you measure setup, not performance. The first three months at most spend levels are net cost; the back half is where the return shows up.
